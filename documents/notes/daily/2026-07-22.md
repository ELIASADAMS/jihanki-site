# 2026-07-22 — ENTERING THE TERRITORY

**Phase:** IV — Entering the Territory; V — Orientation; VI — First Signs

## 01:30 — Arrival near Lake Saiko

Meeting with the mysterious Koba-san, who picked us up by car and drove us to the apartments in Saikoneon.

First visit to conbini 7/11: semi-automatic cash desk, tasty noodles, zundamon and Kagome Kagome juice.

We put together CDs, cassettes and microcassettes and photographed the materials.

### Possible themes

- Guide.
- Gatekeeper.
- The first local figure.
- Smart cash machine.
- Reference to previous works.
- CDs, cassettes, printed materials.

## 02:00 — First Night Walk

The first encounter was with a **jihanki**, a Japanese vending machine. A ghostly view near the hotel and an empty parked bus surrounded it.

The first thing needed to use a jihanki is learning how to operate it and learning about its needs. It would not accept paper money, only coins, and not all coins were accepted.

At night, the vending machine felt less like a shop than an illuminated object existing alone in darkness.

## 14:00 — Discussion with Koba-san

Discussion about our plans and first exploration of the surrounding area.

## 15:00 — Bicycle Ride Around Kawaguchiko

We explored tunnels and discovered that every tunnel has its own name.

Other events:

- First purchase of Seven Stars cigarettes as another checkpoint.
- Meeting the owner of a small local shop.
- The shopkeeper unexpectedly gave us a local juice that we immediately loved.

## 15:45 — Three Jihanki

During the bike ride we came across three vending machines. One was working. Two sold cigarettes and alcohol. Another alcohol vending machine no longer functioned and stood nearby, partially hidden behind flower pots.

The inactive machine looked intentionally concealed rather than simply abandoned. The contrast between active and silent machines made them feel less like infrastructure and more like individual presences.

### Possible themes

- Machines with different states of life.
- Objects that slowly disappear from use without disappearing from the landscape.

## 16:36 — The Wooden Recording Room

We discovered a small detached wooden room that seemed to exist purely as an enclosed space. We immediately decided to use it as a recording location.

### Observations

- It was unclear who had built it or for what purpose.
- It felt like an accidental studio waiting to be used.
- Architecture without an obvious function.

## 18:22 — Supermarket / Dinner

Trip to the larger supermarket with Koba-san. Dinner outdoors in nature. Purchased Orion beer, recommended by a friend back in Moscow.

### Possible theme

- Establishing temporary rituals.

## 20:55 — Long Walk Around Lake Saiko

Eerie empty roads and more jihankis. They were not all the same: different models, colours, sounds, goods and accepted money.

During the walk we fully realised how close we were living to Aokigahara Forest.

Inside the forest we discovered an old public telephone booth.

The first appearance of **Shiki**, local deer, also occurred. We would see them almost every night afterwards.

### Observation

The phone booth appeared almost detached from ordinary infrastructure, standing quietly among the trees.

### Possible themes

- Communication across worlds.
- Forgotten infrastructure.
- Threshold objects.
- The forest beginning to reveal itself.

## 23:00 — Jihanki in the Darkness

Later that night we came across more vending machines standing alone in total darkness. Their glow transformed the surrounding landscape completely.

This became one of the project's first conceptual turning points: the jihanki itself could be interpreted as a supernatural entity.

Julia remembered an old poem, **“Hibangana”**, about the red spider lily, and decided to find the original text and create a song based on it. White lilies were also growing freely along the roads, giving the night air a strong fragrance.

### Possible themes

- Vending machines as contemporary yōkai.
- Islands of light.
- Autonomous presences in the night landscape.
- Flowers, especially lilies.

## 00:39 — First Communal Washing Room Encounter

Encounter with an enormous spider and cricket.

### Possible themes

- Insects as night guardians.
- Telephone booth with strange sounds as a portal.

---

**Project turning point:** ordinary infrastructure began to behave like a cast of entities rather than background scenery.
