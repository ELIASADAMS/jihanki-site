# 2026-08-02 — HANABI

## Hanabi Festival

A night of fireworks.

The festival created another temporary ritual: a crowd of cars gathered, lights appeared in the darkness, the sky changed for a short time, and then everything disappeared just as suddenly.

### Project motifs

- Temporary ritual.
- Lights appearing in darkness.
- Collective gathering.
- Ephemeral events.
- Memory of something that disappears almost immediately.
