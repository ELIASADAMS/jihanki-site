# 2026-08-03 — MACHINES / MEMORY / THE HIDDEN HISTORY

## Museum of Military Aircraft

We visited a museum dedicated to military aircraft.

The atmosphere was distinctly Japanese: a mixture of technology, history, preservation and memory.

Eli bought a book there.

The visit reinforced the idea that Japan's relationship with technology is not only futuristic and digital. It also encompasses preserved machinery, historical memory and artefacts carrying stories from previous generations.

## The Hidden History Near Fuji

Later Koba-san took us to another location: a small memorial and open field where one of Aum Shinrikyo's former largest bases and production facilities once stood.

The site was associated with the organisation's former activities and sarin production before the Tokyo subway attack. The main buildings have since been demolished. Only the landscape remains.

One of the most striking features was a strange localised cloud or mist around Mount Fuji. From a distance it resembled a concentrated cloud of vapour occupying only one section of the landscape. Photographing it was difficult because it behaved more like an atmospheric phenomenon than an ordinary object.

The contrast was unsettling: a place connected with a dark chapter of modern Japanese history now appeared almost empty — a field, a memorial, a mountain producing mist.

### Possible themes

- Invisible histories beneath ordinary landscapes.
- The mountain as a witness.
- Modern yōkai: not supernatural creatures, but human actions that leave traces.
- Places that remember even after buildings disappear.

## Project Note

The expedition began by searching for yōkai as folklore. By the end, the definition had expanded.

A yōkai could be:

- A vending machine glowing alone in the forest.
- A cave with sacred water.
- A forgotten field with a dark history.
- A cloud of mist above Fuji.
- A place where something happened but only the landscape remains.
