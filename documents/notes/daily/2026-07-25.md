# 2026-07-25 — WATER / UNO-MISAKI

## 15:30 — Onsen Yurari

Visit to Onsen Yurari after several days of forests, caves, driving and exploration.

Water had become one of the strongest recurring symbols of the expedition:

- Lake Saiko.
- Lake Motosuko.
- Cave springs.
- Onsen.

## 22:00–00:00 — Shojiko Night Drive / Walk

Night drive and walk around Lake Shoji. White lilies appeared after the rain.

We had our first encounter with **Uno-Misaki Tunnel (宇の岬トンネル)**.

We took photographs inside and recorded the sounds. Julia decided to return to the tunnel to make project videos and record more sounds.

Eli still had some fever, so the walk was short.

On the way back came one of the longest and most detailed encounters with Shiki, with good video documentation.

Julia also remembered that her last performance before going to Japan had been about a deer. She interpreted this as possibly a premonition of Shiki.

### Project material generated

- Uno-Misaki tunnel photographs.
- Tunnel field recordings.
- Plan for a return recording/video session.
- Detailed Shiki documentation.
